Disconnect AWS?

ACT stops reading from AWS and cancels the daily sync. The 19 agents already in ACT stay, with their history, and are marked Source disconnected.

The ACT-ReadOnly role stays in your AWS accounts until you delete the ACT-ReadOnly stack in CloudFormation.
